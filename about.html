About Travelthing

Booking a flight or planning a trip shouldn't mean juggling five different websites and hoping the cheapest fare is also the right one. Travelthing arranges journeys for travellers across Bangladesh — one conversation, one point of contact, from your first question to your ticket in hand.

How we work

Most people don't want to compare eleven booking sites before a trip — they want someone to tell them the best option and handle the paperwork. That's the gap Travelthing fills: you tell us where you're going, and we take it from there.

It starts with a message — most often on WhatsApp. Tell us where you're headed and when, and we come back with real options, not just the lowest number on a screen. Once you decide, we handle the booking, send your ticket directly, and stay reachable if anything about the trip changes.

Why people choose us

Direct communication

One conversation on WhatsApp or Facebook, from enquiry to ticket — no switching between apps or waiting on hold.

Clear pricing

We quote the full price upfront — fare, fees and any service charge together — so what you're told at the start is what you pay at the end.

Support after booking

If a flight is delayed, cancelled or rescheduled, message us and we'll sort out the rebooking — you don't have to call the airline yourself.

Want to know more before you book?

Ask us anything on WhatsApp — we're happy to talk it through first.
